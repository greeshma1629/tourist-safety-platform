# 🛡️ SafeJourney: Tourist Safety Platform

![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue.svg)
![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-1.0.0-green.svg)

SafeJourney is a comprehensive web and mobile platform designed to empower tourists with real-time safety information, emergency tools, and community-driven insights. Traveling to a new city should be exciting, not intimidating—SafeJourney ensures you have a localized safety net right in your pocket.

---

## ✨ Features

*   **🚨 One-Tap SOS:** Instantly triggers an alert sharing your live GPS location with local emergency services and your pre-set trusted contacts.
*   **🗺️ Safety Heatmaps:** Interactive maps highlighting high-risk and low-risk zones based on recent crime data, crowdsourced reports, and time of day.
*   **📍 Safe Routing:** Navigation that prioritizes well-lit, populated, and heavily trafficked streets over the absolute shortest distance.
*   **📞 Localized Emergency Directory:** Automatically updates to show the correct numbers for local police, ambulances, and embassies based on your current GPS coordinates.
*   **💬 Community Watch:** Verified travelers and locals can post real-time updates about scams, transit strikes, or temporary hazards in specific areas.
*   **🌐 Offline Mode:** Cache essential maps and emergency contact information for areas with spotty cellular coverage.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (Example)

*   **Frontend:** React Native (iOS & Android) / React.js (Web Admin)
*   **Backend:** Node.js with Express
*   **Database:** MongoDB & Redis (for real-time caching)
*   **Maps & Routing:** Mapbox API / Google Maps Platform
*   **Authentication:** Firebase Auth / JWT
*   **Real-time Alerts:** Socket.io
